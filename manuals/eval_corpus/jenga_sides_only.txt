Jenga variant: "Outside In"

All the normal rules apply: one hand at a time, take blocks only from below the
highest complete level, and put them on top, completing the top level before a new
one is started. Blocks may only go into the empty places on the top of the tower.

The twist: you may only ever pull a side block. The middle block of a level is
never allowed to be taken.

The game ends when the tower falls.

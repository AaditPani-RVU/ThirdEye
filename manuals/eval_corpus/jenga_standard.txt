JENGA - How to play

Setup: build the tower 18 levels high, three blocks per level, each level at right
angles to the one below.

On your turn, take one block from any level below the highest completed level. Use
only one hand at a time; you may switch hands, but never have two hands touching the
tower at once.

Place the block on the top of the tower, crosswise to the blocks beneath it. Finish
the top level - all three blocks - before starting a new level above it. A block may
only be put down in an empty spot on the top of the tower, never pushed back into
the body of the tower.

The game ends when the tower falls.

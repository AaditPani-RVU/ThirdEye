# Bridge. Baseplate: 12 x 8 studs.
# Coordinates are studs from the bottom-left corner, (x, y); x runs left to right, y away from you.
# Steps 1-2 build the left pillar and steps 3-4 the right pillar. You may build the
# pillars in either order, but each pillar goes bottom brick first.

1. Place a black 2x2 brick with its bottom-left stud at (2, 3).
2. Stack a second black 2x2 brick on top of it.
3. Place a black 2x2 brick with its bottom-left stud at (8, 3).
4. Stack a second black 2x2 brick on top of that one.
5. Lay a red 2x8 brick lengthwise, left to right, on top of both pillars, so that its left end sits on the left pillar and its right end on the right pillar.
6. Stand a yellow 1x2 brick on the bridge, over the fourth column of studs counted from the bridge's left end, with its long side running away from you.

# Totem. Baseplate: 8 x 8 studs.
# Coordinates are studs from the bottom-left corner, (x, y); x runs left to right, y away from you.

1. Place a yellow 2x2 brick in the centre of the plate, with its bottom-left stud at (3, 3).
2. Stack a red 2x2 brick exactly on top of the yellow one.
3. Stack a blue 2x2 brick exactly on top of the red one.
4. Stack a green 2x2 brick exactly on top of the blue one.
5. Finish the totem with a white 2x2 brick on the very top.

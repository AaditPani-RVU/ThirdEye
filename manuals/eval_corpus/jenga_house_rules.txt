Our house rules for Jenga

We play with a few changes to the box rules.

- Use either hand, or both at once if you like. Anything that keeps the game moving.
- To make it harder, the top two complete levels are off limits: you may only take a block from a level below both of them.
- Put the block on top of the tower. As usual, finish the top level before you start a new one, and only place blocks on the top.
- The game is over as soon as the tower collapses.

# A brick wall in running bond. Baseplate: 16 x 16 studs.
# All long bricks lie lengthwise, left to right, along the bottom edge of the plate.

1. Lay a red 2x4 brick in the bottom-left corner.
2. Lay a second red 2x4 brick directly to the right of the first.
3. Lay a third red 2x4 brick directly to the right of the second. This finishes the first course.
4. Start the second course: lay a white 2x4 brick on top of the wall, two studs in from the left end, so it straddles the joint between the first and second red bricks.
5. Lay another white 2x4 brick directly to the right of it on the second course, straddling the joint between the second and third red bricks.
6. Fill the gap at the left end of the second course with a white 2x2 brick.
7. Fill the gap at the right end of the second course with a white 2x2 brick.

# Staircase. Baseplate: 12 x 6 studs.
# Every brick lies lengthwise, left to right, against the bottom edge of the plate.

1. Lay a grey 2x6 brick in the bottom-left corner of the plate.
2. On top of it, lay a grey 2x4 brick flush with its right end.
3. On top of that, place a grey 2x2 brick flush with the right end.
4. Top the staircase with a red 2x2 brick exactly on the grey 2x2.
